# Topic bank

Each topic: the hook, the verified reason, and where the claim comes from. **Re-verify every fact on the day you script it** (web search), and cut anything you can't confirm. Longer draft scripts for batch 2 live in the claude.ai Project doc `claude/batch-2-scripts.md` — use them as a starting point, not as verified copy.

1. **Hole in a ballpoint pen cap** — Hook: "There's a hole in the top of your pen cap. It's not for the ink." Reason: a vented cap lets air through if a child inhales it; UK standard BS 7272 (1990), then ISO 11540; BIC states its caps comply. Draft figure to re-check: nine UK child deaths 1970–84. Source to re-check: Snopes "Are the Holes in Ballpoint Pen Caps to Prevent Choking?". The hole on the barrel is a different job (pressure equalization so ink flows).
2. **Bumps on F and J keys** — Hook: "Two keys on your keyboard have a ridge. Only two." Reason: homing keys for touch typing; index fingers find the home row without looking; numpad 5 has one too. Don't claim a named inventor.
3. **Tabs on the ends of a foil box** — Hook: "You've been fighting the wrong part of this box." Reason: press-in tabs lock the roll's cardboard core so it can't jump out; Reynolds prints "press here to lock roll" on its boxes (re-check current wording).
4. **Hole in the bottom of a padlock** — Hook: "Every padlock has a second hole. Not the keyhole." Reason: drains water (rust, freezing) and is where lock lubricant goes; recommend dry graphite / lock lubricant, not heavy oil.
5. **Overflow hole in a bathroom sink** — Hook: "The hole near the top of your sink isn't doing what you think." Reason: stops flooding AND vents air so a full basin drains faster; also a common source of smells — flush it.
6. **Colored square on a toothpaste tube** — Myth-bust. Hook: "The colored square on your toothpaste means nothing." Reason: "eye mark"/registration mark read by a sensor to cut/fold/seal the tube; not an ingredient code (Colgate explainer, Snopes rated the claim False).
7. **Weep holes in a window frame** — Hook: "People caulk these shut every year. It's an expensive mistake." Reason: slots in vinyl window frames drain water from the bottom track; sealing them traps water that can damage the wall. Advice: keep them clear.

Variety rule: no two consecutive videos from the same room or with the same payoff type.
