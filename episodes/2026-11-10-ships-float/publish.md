## Caption
A cargo ship is made of steel, almost eight times denser than water, and it still floats. The trick is the shape: the hull is mostly air, so the ship sinks only until the water it pushes aside weighs as much as the ship. Load it and it sits deeper, which is why there is a load line painted on the hull. Sail it empty and it pumps seawater into ballast tanks to sit low enough. And watertight walls keep one hole from sinking the whole ship. Would you cross an ocean on a ship that's mostly air?
