## Caption (all platforms)
Every escalator step has two pairs of wheels. The front pair is hooked to the chain, the back pair rolls in a second track that's shaped to keep the step level the whole way up. At the top, the tracks level off and the grooves slide into the comb plate. And yes, "escalator" used to be a brand name.
