## YouTube description
How It's Built #32
How a subway station is built under a busy street with cut and cover: slurry walls first (a clamshell bucket digs a trench kept open by bentonite slurry, a steel cage goes in, concrete is placed from the bottom and pushes the slurry out), then a road deck on steel beams so traffic keeps moving, utilities hung from the beams, digging under the deck with steel struts, the station built from the floor up, struts out, backfill, street rebuilt. Depth (68 ft) and wall thickness (3.5 ft) from the Second Avenue Subway's 96th St station in New York. Simplified illustration; station length shortened.
