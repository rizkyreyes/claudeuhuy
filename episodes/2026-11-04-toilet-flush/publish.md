## Caption
Your toilet doesn't push the water out. It sucks it out. Hidden in the base is a bent pipe called the trapway, and the water in the bowl can only sit as high as the top of that bend, which is also what keeps sewer gas out. When you flush, the tank dumps in through the rim holes and a jet, the bend fills up completely, and the water falling down the drain pulls the bowl after it. That's a siphon, and the gurgle at the end is air breaking it. Then the float and fill valve refill the tank, and a small tube refills the bowl so the seal comes back. Have you ever had to flush with a bucket?
