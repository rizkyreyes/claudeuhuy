## YouTube description
How It's Built
Where your shower water really goes. Under the drain, the pipe bends into a U (the P-trap) that always holds a few inches of water: that seal keeps sewer gas out of your bathroom. The trap arm slopes about a quarter inch per foot, a vent pipe through the roof stops rushing water from sucking the trap dry, and the shower joins the toilet and sinks in one building drain out to the city sewer or a septic tank. If a shower goes unused for weeks, the seal evaporates and the bathroom starts to smell: run the water for a minute. Simplified illustration, pipe sizes exaggerated.
