water-tower (3d-20), facts checked 9 Nov 2026 (cloud run, search excerpts; practical.engineering fetch is blocked in the cloud network).
Every claim has two agreeing sources or an official / encyclopedic one. Wording kept general where sources differ.
Cut / not claimed: a "typical" tank size (sources give 50,000 to 4 million gallons), "sized for one day of demand" (one secondary source only,
a patent says about half a day), a fixed household psi range (sources give 35 / 40-80 / 45-80 / 50-100), "built on the highest ground",
the share of the tank kept for fire flow, how long gravity alone lasts in an outage (VO says "for a while").

Water towers don't pump water into homes; the height (gravity / hydrostatic pressure) pressurizes the mains
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_tower ("The height of the tower provides the pressure for the water supply system"; supplies water during power outages because it relies on hydrostatic pressure produced by elevation)
  - https://people.howstuffworks.com/water.htm
  - https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/64577/how-do-water-towers-work
A tank can sit more than a hundred feet up (many elevated tanks are 100-200 ft; e.g. Keokuk, Iowa 150 ft, General Hancock tower 175 ft; vendor overflow heights up to 200 ft)
  - https://www.worldatlas.com/bodies-of-water/the-largest-water-towers-in-the-united-states.html
  - https://www.cstindustries.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Aquastore-Composite-Elevated-Tanks-Brochure-2021_web.pdf
  - https://www.caldwelltanks.com/tank_types/multi-column-elevated-storage-tank-leg
If pressure drops too low, some states require a boil-water advisory (Wikipedia: below 20 psi)
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_tower
Every foot of water height adds about 0.43 (0.433) psi; 2.31 ft per psi
  - https://www.ayresassociates.com/under-pressure-how-municipal-water-commutes-to-work/
  - https://ny-engineers.com/blog/how-are-water-towers-used
  - https://people.howstuffworks.com/water.htm
A hundred feet of water gives about 43 psi (100 x 0.433 = 43.3; one source: "A 100 ft column causes about 43.3 psi at ground level")
  - https://www.ayresassociates.com/under-pressure-how-municipal-water-commutes-to-work/ (and arithmetic from the line above)
The tank's width (diameter) doesn't matter, only the height of the water column ("Only the tank's height ... matters for psi / feet of range calculations - not its diameter")
  - https://www.ayresassociates.com/under-pressure-how-municipal-water-commutes-to-work/
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrostatics (pressure depends on depth, not on the shape of the container)
In many towers one large pipe (riser) carries water up into the tank and lets it flow back out to the network (some designs use separate inlet/outlet, hence "in many towers")
  - https://pttg.com/blogs/everything-you-need-to-know-about-how-water-towers-work.php
  - https://www.redzet.lv/en/info/Water_tower (mirror of the Wikipedia text)
Pumps fill the tank at night when demand is low; the level falls during the daytime peaks and the tank covers them
  - https://people.howstuffworks.com/water.htm
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_tower ("The water level in the tower typically falls during the peak usage hours of the day, and then a pump fills it back up during the night")
So pumps can be sized for average demand rather than peak demand
  - https://people.howstuffworks.com/water.htm
  - https://www.h2g2.com/edited_entry/A13346633
In a power outage, gravity keeps water flowing for a while (cannot supply for long without the pumps refilling)
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_tower
  - https://www.slashgear.com/2182926/water-towers-keep-water-pressure-high-store-for-emergencies/
Part of the stored water is held back for firefighting
  - https://www.aacounty.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/heritage-harbour-tower-ewst-poster.pdf (county public works: fire storage = water for firefighting beyond peak demand)
  - https://www.slashgear.com/2182926/water-towers-keep-water-pressure-high-store-for-emergencies/
  - https://people.howstuffworks.com/water.htm
An overflow pipe dumps excess water if the tank gets too full
  - https://pttg.com/blogs/everything-you-need-to-know-about-how-water-towers-work.php
  - https://www.caldwelltanks.com/tank_types/multi-column-elevated-storage-tank-leg ("overflow" heights listed per tank)
