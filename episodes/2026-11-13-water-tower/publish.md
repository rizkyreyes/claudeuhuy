## Caption
Water towers don't pump water into your house. Gravity does. Every foot of water above your tap adds about 0.43 psi, so a hundred feet gives about 43 psi, and the tank's width doesn't matter at all. Pumps fill the tank at night, it drains back down the same pipe during the morning rush, and part of it is held back for firefighters. Would you climb the ladder to the top of one?
