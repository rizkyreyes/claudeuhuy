A backyard pool starts as a hole, a little bigger than the pool. Then comes a cage of steel bars, the plumbing, and concrete blasted onto the steel with a hose: gunite if it's a dry mix, shotcrete if it's wet. Tile, coping and a coat of plaster seal it, and the pump keeps the water moving through the filter. One catch: drain it when the groundwater is high and the whole pool can float up out of the ground. Would you pick a concrete pool or an above-ground one?
