- Build order of a gunite / shotcrete pool: layout + excavation, steel (rebar) cage incl. steps and benches, plumbing rough-in
  through the steel before the concrete, gunite/shotcrete sprayed at high velocity onto the steel and troweled smooth,
  curing kept moist, waterline tile + coping, interior plaster finish, then filled
  -> https://ssgpools.com/gunite-pools-your-complete-resource-guide/ ; https://projul.com/blog/construction-pool-spa-construction-guide/ ;
     https://www.cotality.com/resources/gunite-swimming-pools-wildfire-damage-recovery-and-resilience ; https://projul.com/blog/construction-swimming-pool-aquatic-facility-guide
- The hole is over-dug beyond the shell (room for plumbing, rebar and shotcrete)
  -> https://projul.com/blog/construction-pool-spa-construction-guide/
- Gunite = dry mix (water added at the nozzle), shotcrete = wet mix
  -> https://ssgpools.com/gunite-pools-your-complete-resource-guide/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shotcrete ; https://cameo.mfa.org/wiki/Gunite
- Concrete is strong in compression and weak in tension; steel rebar carries the tension
  -> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebar ; https://practical.engineering/blog/2018/8/1/why-does-concrete-need-reinforcement
- Main drain at the deep end + skimmer at the waterline (suction), pump pushes water through the filter, return jets send it back
  -> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pool_skimmer ; https://www.riverpoolsandspas.com/blog/how-do-pool-pump-and-filter-systems-work ;
     https://www.swimuniversity.com/pool-anatomy/ ; https://poolonomics.com/parts-of-a-pool/
- Plaster is the layer that makes the shell watertight; bare concrete is porous and lets moisture seep through slowly
  -> https://bluehaven.com/?p=14360 ; https://www.troublefreepool.com/goto/post?id=2173433
- An empty pool can be lifted ("popped") out of the ground by buoyancy when groundwater is high
  -> https://degenkolb.com/ideas/when-swimming-pools-pop/ ; https://www.envistaforensics.com/knowledge-center/insights/articles/floating-swimming-pools-part-i/ ;
     https://geoprac.net/2015/08/florida-pool-pushed-out-of-ground-after-heavy-rains/
- A hydrostatic relief valve (often in the main drain) opens and lets groundwater in when the outside pressure is higher
  -> https://watershapes.com/singing-the-relief-valve-blues/ ; https://www.sunplay.com/blog/faqs-hydrostatic-relief-plugs/ ; https://www.1stdirectpools.com/1-5-hydro-relief-valve-abs/
- History: Carl Akeley (taxidermist, Field Columbian Museum, Chicago) developed the cement gun to replaster the museum's facade;
  patent No. 991814 (1911 most cited), "gunite" coined later. "Over a hundred years ago" is true for every date in the sources.
  -> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shotcrete ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Akeley ; https://shotcrete.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/2017Fal_Morgan-Bernard.pdf ;
     https://shotcrete.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/2015Sum_Samuels-Drakeley.pdf ; https://watershapes.com/?p=30671
