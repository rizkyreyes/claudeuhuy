Episode 3d-04 undersea-tunnel: claim -> source. Claims locked from concordant search-result excerpts of these pages
(this run did not open publisher pages directly).

- A TBM builds the tunnel lining as it advances: the segment erector places precast concrete segments in a ring;
  the machine does not dig while a ring is being built
  -> https://t2d.sa.gov.au/construction/tunnel-boring-machine ; https://crosscut.com/2013/07/diggng-it-tunnel-geeks-watching-bertha ;
     https://patents.google.com/patent/US3859810A
- The rotating cutterhead digs with cutting teeth and disc cutters; disc cutters roll over the rock, are pressed against the face
  and crush it, cracks join up and chips break off
  -> https://t2d.sa.gov.au/construction/tunnel-boring-machine ; https://ntnuopen.ntnu.no/ntnu-xmlui/handle/11250/2615083 ;
     https://www.engineering.org.cn/engi/EN/1159834608697140017
- "Turns slowly": full-scale tests show low cutterhead RPM breaks rock better (no RPM number is said in the VO)
  -> https://nff.no/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/04/15-Vassenden.pdf
- Slurry shield: pressurized bentonite/clay suspension in the excavation chamber counteracts earth and groundwater pressure and
  supports the face; used in unstable ground with high groundwater pressure; muck mixes into the slurry and is pumped to a
  separation plant on the surface, which cleans the slurry so it is recycled
  -> https://tunnel.ita-aites.org/en/how-to-go-underground/construction-methods/mechanized-tunnelling/slurry-shield ;
     https://trenchlesspedia.com/definition/2552/slurry-tunnel-boring-machine-slurry-tbm ; https://tunnelpro.it/en/slurry-tbm
- Segments are erected inside the tail shield; hydraulic thrust jacks push on the newly built ring to move the machine forward
  -> https://www.mydigitalpublication.com/article/Design%2C+Manufacturing+and+Construction+of+Precast+Concrete+Tunnel+Segments/4402305/770950/article.html ;
     https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10706-023-02518-1 ; https://crosscut.com/2013/07/diggng-it-tunnel-geeks-watching-bertha
- An annular void is left between the segments and the ground; it is filled with grout
  -> https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10706-023-02518-1 ; https://concrete.org/portals/0/files/pdf/webinars/ws_S18_Bakhshi.pdf
- Gaskets (EPDM rubber) frame each segment; the concrete is virtually watertight, the joints are what must be sealed; gaskets
  only work in compression; bolts compress them
  -> https://legacy.csce.ca/elf/apps/CONFERENCEVIEWER/conferences/2018/pdfs/Paper_GC5_0607023944.pdf ;
     https://www.si-eng.org/post/gaskets-for-tunnel-segments ; https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/9/4556/html
- Channel Tunnel: 11 TBMs in total
  -> https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/101994486 ; https://www.discoveryuk.com/?p=23587015
- Channel Tunnel drives kept within the chalk marl, which is nearly impermeable and relatively easy to dig
  -> https://geolsoc.org.uk/GeositesChannelTunnel ; https://www.vinci-construction-projets.com/es/realisations/tunnel-sous-la-manche/
- Service tunnel breakthrough (the two sides met): 1 December 1990
  -> https://tunneltalk.com/Channel-Tunnel-Dec10-20-years-on.php ; https://www.24-7pressrelease.com/press-release/179853/eurotunnel-celebrates-20-year-anniversary-of-linking-england-and-france
- When the drives met, the French TBMs were dismantled; the British ones were diverted into the rock and buried/abandoned
  -> https://mirror2.polsri.ac.id/wiki/wp/c/Channel_Tunnel.htm (Wikipedia mirror) ; https://www.robbinstbm.com/projects/the-channel-tunnel/
