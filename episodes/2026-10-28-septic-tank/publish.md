## Caption (all platforms)
A septic tank never fills up because it's already full. Gravity sorts the wastewater: solids sink as sludge, grease floats as scum, and the clearer liquid in the middle sits right at the outlet pipe. Every gallon that comes in pushes one gallon out to the drainfield, where the soil does most of the cleaning. Bacteria can't eat all the sludge, so the tank still gets pumped every three to five years. Is your home on septic or on the sewer?
