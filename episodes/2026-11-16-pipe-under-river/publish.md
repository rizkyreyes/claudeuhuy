## Caption
If you need a pipe across a river, you don't dig through it. You drill under it and steer the whole way. A rig pushes a steel rod in at an angle, a slanted tip lets the crew turn it, a transmitter tells them where it is, then a reamer widens the hole, drilling mud holds the walls up, and the whole pipe gets pulled back through in one piece. It's called horizontal directional drilling. How many pipes do you think are under the river closest to you?
