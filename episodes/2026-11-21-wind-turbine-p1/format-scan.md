## Applied element
**Loop ending + build-up from a hole**: the video opens on the finished turbine standing on its cut-open foundation ("A buried slab of concrete and a pile of dirt."), rewinds to bare ground, builds it step by step (hole, rebar, bolts, pour, backfill, tower, nacelle, blades), and the last line "And every ton of it rests on" cuts hard into the first line again; the camera's last frame equals frame 1.
