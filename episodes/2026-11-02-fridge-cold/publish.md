## Caption (all platforms)
Your fridge doesn't make cold. It moves heat out. A sealed loop of tubing runs through the walls, filled with a refrigerant that boils at very low temperatures. The compressor squeezes the gas and makes it hot, the coils on the back dump that heat into your kitchen, and a very thin tube drops the pressure so the refrigerant boils below freezing inside the freezer coils, pulling the warmth out of the air. A fan and a small flap send the cold air around, a thermostat switches it all on and off, and a heater quietly melts the frost now and then. Did you know the back of your fridge was a heater?
