## Applied element
**"Follow one drop" journey with a what-if in the middle**: the camera follows the same cyan water beads from the lake, down the penstock, around the spiral casing, through the gates and the runner, up the shaft into the generator, and back out to the river; at the midpoint the flow is stopped (gates slam) and the what-if plays out (water hammer, 1950 burst), the same stake mechanic as the subway cave-in.
Why: a single path is easy to follow at a glance (no jumps between unrelated parts), every beat ends with "and then the water...", which pulls viewers to the next step; the danger beat in the middle breaks the explainer rhythm right where retention usually sags (~35-45 s).
