## Applied element
**"Tiny number vs giant structure" contrast hook + a reveal of what you can't see**: open on the smallest possible number ("less than an inch") over the largest possible structure (a 160 ft, three-tier bridge), then keep swapping scale: 30 miles, an inch, 6-ton stones, a hidden channel underground, a layer of limestone. This is the same scale-contrast pattern as the 14 Nov escalation rule ("small everyday thing -> giant engineering"), in reverse.
Why: the hook is a concrete, checkable, surprising number on screen in the first 2 s; each beat answers "so how?" with something hidden (underground, inside the pipe, inside the channel), which suits the cutaway format; the what-if (wrong slope, flow stops) puts a stake into the first 15 s.
