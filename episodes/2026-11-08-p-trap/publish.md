## Caption
The pipe under your sink is always full of water, and that's on purpose. The U-bend is called a P-trap. Most of the water drains on, but the last bit stays in the bottom of the bend and makes a seal, so the sewer gas behind it (that rotten egg smell) can't get into your bathroom. The vent pipe up through the roof lets air in so a big rush of water doesn't siphon the seal out. It also catches rings you drop. If a guest bathroom smells, run the water for a few seconds. Have you ever dropped something down the sink?
