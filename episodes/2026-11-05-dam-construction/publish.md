## Caption
To build a dam, you first have to move the river. Crews blast tunnels through the canyon walls (Hoover Dam got four), then dump rock into the river to make a cofferdam, and within a day the river turns into the tunnels. A second cofferdam goes downstream and the riverbed between them drains. They dig to solid bedrock and pump liquid cement into deep holes to make a hidden curtain under the dam. The concrete goes up in blocks with cooling pipes inside, because one single pour at Hoover Dam would have taken about 125 years to cool. Would you walk across a dam that tall?
