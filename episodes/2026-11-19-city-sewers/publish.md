## Caption
If you flush a toilet, gravity does almost all the work. The pipe leaves your house tilted, often a quarter inch every foot, runs under the yard to the sewer main under the street, and every house on the block ties in. Manholes sit wherever the pipe turns, changes slope or gets bigger. When the pipe gets too deep, a lift station pumps it up and gravity takes over again, all the way to the treatment plant.
