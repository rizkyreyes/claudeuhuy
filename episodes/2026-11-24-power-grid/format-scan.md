## Applied element
**Reverse-journey with a payoff loop**: open where the viewer is (the outlet in their wall), state the shocking number (hundreds of thousands of volts), stake (it would fry your wiring), then follow the power from the plant back to that same outlet, and loop the last line ("...none of that high voltage ever reaches you, even though") into the hook. Three gold markers on "three times" give a mini countdown structure for the stages.
Why: "it's in your wall right now" makes a big-system topic personal (home-item surprise + big infrastructure, both of our winners), and the journey ends on the exact first frame.
