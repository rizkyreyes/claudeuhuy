## Caption
A nuclear power plant is basically a giant kettle. One uranium pellet holds about as much energy as a ton of coal, the water around the core hits about 600°F but never boils because it's squeezed to about 2,250 psi, and that heat boils a second loop of water into steam that spins the turbine. The cloud over the towers? Just water vapor. Would you live next to one?
