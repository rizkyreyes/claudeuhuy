## 01-gas-gauge-arrow.jpg
Next time you pull up to the pump, look at the little arrow next to the gas icon on your dash. It points to the side your fuel door is on. Handy in a rental car you have never driven before.
#cartips #lifehacks #everydaytips

## 02-pot-handle-hole.jpg
That hole at the end of your pot handle isn't only for hanging it up. Slide your stirring spoon through it and the spoon rests over the pot, so the drips go back in instead of on the stove.
#kitchentips #cookinghacks #everydaytips

## 03-tape-measure-slot.jpg
The little slot in the metal hook on your tape measure fits over a nail or screw head. Hook it on, walk the tape out, and you can measure a whole wall without someone holding the end.
#diytips #homeimprovement #everydaytips

## 04-paint-can-rim.jpg
Paint filling up the groove around the can every time you pour? Tap a few small nail holes into the bottom of that groove. The paint drains back into the can and the lid seals clean.
#paintingtips #diytips #homehacks

## 05-skip-the-rinse.jpg
You can skip rinsing plates before they go in the dishwasher. Scrape the food into the trash and load them. Consumer Reports says dishwasher detergent cleans better when it has some food to work on, and you save the water.
#cleaningtips #kitchentips #everydaytips

## 06-butter-wrapper-lines.jpg
The lines printed on a stick of butter wrapper are tablespoon marks. One line, one tablespoon. Cut straight through the wrapper and skip the measuring spoon.
#bakingtips #kitchenhacks #everydaytips

## 07-hanger-notches.jpg
Those little notches on the shoulders of a hanger are made for straps. Hook the thin straps of a tank top or dress into them and it stays put instead of sliding to the closet floor.
#closetorganization #laundrytips #everydaytips

## 08-sticky-note-peel.jpg
If your sticky notes keep curling up and falling off, try this. Peel the note sideways, from the side, instead of pulling it up from the bottom. It stays flat and sticks better.
#officetips #lifehacks #everydaytips

## 09-sheets-in-pillowcase.jpg
Fold your sheet set and tuck it inside one of its own pillowcases. Everything from that set stays together, and the linen closet looks tidy without any bins.
#homeorganization #cleaningtips #everydaytips

## 10-leftovers-ring.jpg
Leftovers hot on the edges and cold in the middle? Spread the food in a ring on the plate and leave the center empty. The microwave heats it much more evenly.
#kitchentips #cookinghacks #everydaytips
