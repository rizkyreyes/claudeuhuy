## Kitchen, bathroom, laundry, bedroom, clothing, cleaning, packaging

1. **Metal mesh dots in the microwave door** (slug: microwave-door-mesh, room: kitchen, payoff: safety). Hook: "The dots on your microwave door are a shield you can see through." Reason: the door's metal screen has holes much smaller than the ~12 cm wavelength of the oven's 2.45 GHz microwaves, so to them it acts like solid metal, while visible light (much shorter wavelength) passes through. FDA rule 21 CFR 1030.10 caps leakage at 5 mW/cm² about 2 inches from the surface over the oven's lifetime. Sources: https://www.sfu.ca/phys/346/121/resources/physics_of_microwave_ovens.pdf , https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/resources-you-radiation-emitting-products/microwave-ovens. Confidence: high
