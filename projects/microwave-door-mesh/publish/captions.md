Title: Why Does Your Microwave Door Have Tiny Dots? #shorts

Description:
That mesh on your microwave door is a metal screen with holes smaller than the microwaves, so the waves can't get through. Microwave waves are about 12 centimeters long. The holes are only a couple of millimeters wide, so to the waves the screen acts like solid metal. Visible light is far shorter, so it passes through and you can watch your food. A federal rule limits leakage to 5 milliwatts per square centimeter, measured about 2 inches from the oven, for its whole life.
