**Description:**
That tiny hole in a pot lid is a steam vent. It lets a little steam out so pressure doesn't build up, the lid doesn't rattle, and the pot is less likely to spill over. The thermometer story went viral, but only some slow cooker models have a hole made for a probe. Most lids just vent steam, so leave it open.
