**Title:** Why Does a Pen Cap Have a Tiny Hole? #shorts

**Description:**
That hole in your pen cap isn't for the ink — it's there so a child can still breathe if they swallow it. A UK safety standard from 1990 (BS 7272) made it mandatory after nine children died choking on pen caps between 1970 and 1984.
