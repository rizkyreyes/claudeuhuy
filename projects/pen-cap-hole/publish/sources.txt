3. UK safety standard BS 7272 (1990) for writing instrument caps; later superseded by international
   standard ISO 11540. Cited in the Snopes/AP reporting above (9 UK child deaths 1970-1984 from
   choking on pen caps; BIC began perforating Bic Cristal caps in 1990 and applied the design worldwide,
   quote from BIC spokeswoman Astrid Canevet).

   flow as it's consumed — general pen-engineering explanation, corroborated across multiple patent
   filings and explainer sources (e.g. Now I Know, "The Hole Truth About Ballpoint Pens",
