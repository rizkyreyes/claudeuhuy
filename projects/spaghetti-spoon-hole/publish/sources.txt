Facts (publisher pages not opened; locked from concordant search excerpts, 18 Oct 2026):
- Snopes rated the claim that the center hole of a spaghetti spoon measures one serving "Mixture" (Madison Dapcevich, 20 Jan 2024): designs vary, holes aren't standardized, some spoons are made for portion control, mostly the hole is for draining water: https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/holes-pasta-spoons/ (also republished on Yahoo)
- Registered dietitian Melissa Prest told Snopes that loosely gathering noodles through the hole gives about one serving, tight bundling a bit more. A typical serving is 2 oz uncooked spaghetti or one cup cooked (US National Library of Medicine, quoted by Snopes).
- The measuring claim went viral from a June 2016 Imgur post and was repeated by outlets including Food Network and Today (Today piece: https://www.today.com/food/pasta-hack-here-s-why-your-spaghetti-spoon-has-hole-t100560). Metro noted some pasta spoons have small holes or slits too small to measure.
