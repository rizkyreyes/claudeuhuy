Title: Why Does Your Iron Have a Notch at the Tip? #shorts

Description:
That notch at the tip of your iron is a groove for buttons. Slide the tip in beside a button and it settles into the groove, so the hot plate stays off it and you can press the fabric right up to its edge. Plastic buttons can melt if the iron sits too close, so iron around them, never over them.
