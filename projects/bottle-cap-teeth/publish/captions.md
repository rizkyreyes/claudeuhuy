## YouTube
Title: Why Does a Bottle Cap Have 21 Teeth? #shorts

Description:
A standard crown cap has 21 teeth. William Painter's first one, patented in 1892 in Baltimore, had 24. The teeth are the ruffled edge that gets crimped over the lip of the glass, and that grip holds the pressure in. You'll hear that 21 works because it's a multiple of three, but that story mostly comes from cap makers and I couldn't find a source that proves it.
