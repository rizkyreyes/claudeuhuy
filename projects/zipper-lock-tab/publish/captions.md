## YouTube
Title: Why Does Your Zipper Keep Sliding Down? #shorts

Description:
Most zippers on clothes have an auto-lock slider. A tiny pin inside the slider drops between the teeth when the pull tab lies flat, so the zipper can't creep open. Lift the tab and the pin lifts out of the way. If your fly keeps sliding down, fold the tab flat after you zip up. Forcing the slider while the tab is flat can wear the pin down over time.
