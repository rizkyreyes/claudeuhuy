Why Does Toothpaste Have a Colored Square on the Tube? #shorts

Description:

That colored square on your toothpaste tube isn't an ingredient code. Green does not mean natural and black does not mean chemicals, and fact checkers have rated that claim false. It's an eye mark. A light sensor on the tube-making machine reads it to know where to print, cut and seal the tube. The color just has to stand out so the sensor can see it. To know what's in your toothpaste, read the ingredient list on the tube or box.
