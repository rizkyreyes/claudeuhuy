# Sources — sink-overflow

Facts verified 2026-09-28 via web search (WebSearch tool).

1. Overflow-hole purpose #1 (spill prevention): if a faucet is left running or a
   drain clogs, the overflow channel diverts water back down before it spills
   over the rim, buying time before a flood.
   - "What Is The Hole At The Top Of Your Bathroom Sink For?" — House Digest
     https://www.housedigest.com/1801300/hole-top-bathroom-sink-use-explained-what-for/
   - "What Is The Hole At The Top Of Your Bathroom Sink For?" — Yahoo Lifestyle
     https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/hole-top-bathroom-sink-163000937.html
   - "Function and Cleaning of a Sink Overflow Hole" — Merry Maids
     https://merrymaids.com/blog/function-and-cleaning-sink-overflow-hole

2. Overflow-hole purpose #2 (drain speed / airflow): the hole lets air into the
   drain channel as the sink empties, breaking the vacuum that would otherwise
   slow the water down, so a full basin drains faster and smoother.
   - Merry Maids (above)
   - "Are Overflow Holes in Bathroom Basins Necessary?" — The Plumbette
     https://www.theplumbette.com.au/building-and-renovation/are-overflow-holes-in-bathroom-basins-necessary/

3. Not a plumbing-code requirement: no plumbing code mandates an overflow hole
   on a bathroom sink in most jurisdictions — it's a voluntary manufacturer
   feature, not a universal legal requirement (which is why some vessel/vanity
   sinks skip it).
   - "Overflow holes in bathroom sinks - required by code?" — Houzz Forum
     https://www.houzz.com/discussions/2815550/overflow-holes-in-bathroom-sinks-required-by-code
   - IAPMO Code Spotlight (March 2018), "Not all plumbing fixtures are
     required to have an overflow."
     https://forms.iapmo.org/email_marketing/codespotlight/2018/mar8.htm

4. Maintenance note: soap, toothpaste, and skin oils migrate into the overflow
   channel over time and build up, becoming a spot for bacteria/mold/biofilm
   if never cleaned.
   - House Digest (above)
   - "The Purpose and Cleaning of a Sink Overflow Hole" — Badger Luxe Cleaning
     https://badgerluxecleaning.com/purpose-and-cleaning-of-a-sink-overflow-hole/
