Title: Premium Gas Won't Help a Car That Takes Regular

In 2016 AAA tested cars built for regular gas with regular and with premium. The premium gave no extra horsepower, no better fuel economy and no fewer tailpipe emissions. If your owner's manual says premium is required, use it. If it only recommends it, regular may cost you a little power.
