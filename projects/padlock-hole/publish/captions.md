**Title:** Why Does a Padlock Have a Hole in the Bottom? #shorts

**Description:**
That tiny hole under a padlock drains water so the lock doesn't rust or crack when it freezes, and it doubles as the spot to drop lubricant when the shackle sticks. Skip the household oil though — locksmiths reach for dry graphite or PTFE instead, since oil turns into a gritty paste over time.
