Title: Why Does Your Rearview Mirror Have a Tab Underneath?
(vidIQ short-form scores: this 90, "That Tab Under Your Rearview Mirror Is a Night Mode" 83)

YouTube description:
Your rearview mirror is a wedge of glass with a silver coating on the back. The plain front glass reflects only about 4 percent of the light, so flipping the tab to tilt the silver away from the road cuts headlight glare. Flip it back in daylight. Many newer cars use glass that darkens by itself instead.
