Title: Why Does a Trash Can Have Holes Near the Top? #shorts

Description:
Those holes near the top let air slip in under the bag. Without them, a full bag seals against the walls and the can sucks it back down like a vacuum, so you end up yanking and ripping it. Some cans use molded channels inside the wall instead, same idea. Rubbermaid says the channels on its commercial vented cans make liner removal up to 50% easier.
