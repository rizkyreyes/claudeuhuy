# Sources: hair-dryer-plug-block

Facts verified 2026-10-03 via WebSearch. Direct fetches of govinfo.gov, cpsc.gov and ul.com were blocked by the
network proxy, so every claim is locked from concordant search-result excerpts of several CPSC pages and
manufacturer manuals, not from full-page reads.

1. The block is an integral immersion-protection device in the attachment plug (ALCI, appliance leakage circuit interrupter), a relatively large block-shaped plug with Test and Reset pushbuttons. LOCKED
   - Federal Register 28 Jun 2011 (CPSC hair dryer notice) https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2011-06-28/html/2011-15981.htm
   - CPSC Hand Supported Hair Dryers https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Hand-Supported-Hair-Dryers
   - CPSC 1990 release https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/1990/Some-New-Hair-Dryers-Offer-Improved-Electrocution-Protection-When-Immersed-In-Water
   - UL, "What is the reset button on your hair dryer" https://www.ul.com/insights/what-reset-button-your-hair-dryer-and-when-use-it
2. How it works: detects a change/leakage in the current (e.g., appliance immersed in water) and cuts power within milliseconds; the Test button simulates a leakage current. LOCKED (UL page excerpt; CPSC FR notice; manufacturer manuals). Wording used: "a few thousandths of a second", "compares current out and back" (simplified explanation of leakage detection).
3. Test: press Reset, press Test, the Reset button should pop out; if it does not, stop using the dryer. LOCKED (UL excerpt; Remington hair dryer manuals e.g. https://s7cdn.spectrumbrands.com/~/media/PersonalCare/Remington2017/Files/Manuals/Womens/Dryers/D8700SP%20PROtect%20Hair%20Dryer%20Manual.pdf ; BaBylissPRO manuals).
4. Before protection devices, an average of about 18 hand-held hair dryer electrocutions a year (CPSC recall notices give 17-18; FR notice says 15-20); about half of victims were children under 10. LOCKED. Video says "about eighteen a year" and "about half were small children".
   - https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/1996/cpsc-and-bills-dollar-stores-recall-electric-hair-dryers ; https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/1997/cpsc-and-mbr-industries-inc-recall-hair-dryers ; https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/pdfs/blk_pdf_hairdryers.pdf
5. UL 859: the 1987 revision required protection when switched off; since 1 Jan 1991 protection must work switched on or off. LOCKED (CPSC 1989 and 1990 releases, CPSC recall notices, FR 2011).
6. Since immersion protection devices were introduced, deaths fell to two in 1992. LOCKED (same sentence appears in several CPSC recall notices from 1995-1997).
7. Bathroom manuals: unplug the dryer after use in a bathroom; do not use near bathtubs, sinks or water. LOCKED (BaBylissPRO and Remington manuals).

"UL 859 required it from 1987 and 1991" kept only in the corrected form above (1987 = off position only).
Not claimed: any brand, any person, "saved lives" is the hook framing supported by the 1992 drop.
