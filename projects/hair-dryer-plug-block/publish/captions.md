Title: Why Does a Hair Dryer Plug Have a Big Block on It? #shorts

Description:
That chunky block on a hair dryer plug is a leakage current protection device, often called an ALCI. It watches the current going to the dryer, and if some of it starts leaking out another way, it cuts the power almost instantly. Press reset, then test, and the reset button should pop out. If it doesn't, stop using that dryer. The CPSC says hair dryers once caused about 18 electrocutions a year in the U.S., and it counted two deaths in 1992 after this protection became standard.
