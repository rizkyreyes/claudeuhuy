Description:
That raised 57 on the neck of a glass ketchup bottle is the spot Heinz says to tap. Tilt the bottle to about 45 degrees and give it a firm tap right where the glass narrows, and the ketchup comes out easier. It works because ketchup is a shear-thinning fluid. Left alone it's thick, but force makes it runnier. The 57 itself was never a count of products. Henry Heinz picked it in 1896 from his lucky number five and his wife's lucky seven.
