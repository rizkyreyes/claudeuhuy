HE means high efficiency. Those washers use a lot less water, so suds have nowhere to go, and regular detergent can make the machine pause or add extra rinses. HE detergent is made to foam less. The logo isn't a guarantee, though, and even HE detergent can over-suds if you pour too much, so measure the cap.
