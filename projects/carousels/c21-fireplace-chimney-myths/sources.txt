# c21 fireplace and chimney myths: fact -> source (checked 21 Nov 2026, cloud run; checked on search excerpts, csia.org, usfa.fema.gov, cpsc.gov and epa.gov are not reachable from the sandbox)
1 Creosote sweeping logs / chemical cleaners are not an adequate substitute for mechanical chimney cleaning and annual inspection
  CSIA FAQ: https://csia.org/faq.html
  CSIA page on the sweeping log (does not take the place of inspection and professional cleaning): https://csia.org/creosote_sweeping_log.html
2 Don't burn wrapping paper in the fireplace: wrappings can ignite suddenly and burn intensely, a flash fire may result
  CPSC holiday safety tips (1998): https://www.cpsc.gov/en/Newsroom/News-Releases/1998/CPSC-Releases-Safety-Tips-for-the-Holiday-Season/
  CPSC holiday decoration tips (2002): https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2002/CPSC-Releases-Holiday-Decoration-Safety-Tips-for-Avoiding-Fires-and-Injuries
3 Ashes stay hot: put them in a metal container with a lid, outside, at least 10 feet from the home ("for days": Fairfax County says wait at least 4 days before dumping)
  U.S. Fire Administration fireplace/woodstove safety card: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/publications/fireplace_woodstove_safety_card.pdf
  Fairfax County Fire and Rescue: https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/fire-ems/sites/fire-ems/files/Assets/Documents/PDF/fireplace_and_ashes_safety_tips.pdf
4 Season firewood at least 6 months (softwood) / 12 months (hardwood); burns best under 20% moisture; check with a moisture meter (about $15-40)
  EPA Burn Wise best practices (program archived Oct 2025, guidance still online): https://www.epa.gov/burnwise/best-wood-burning-practices
  EPA Burn Wise moisture meter: https://epa.gov/burnwise/test-your-wood-moisture-meter
5 Chimneys, fireplaces and vents should be inspected at least once a year (NFPA 211, cited by CSIA, applies to gas systems too); CPSC: yearly inspection should check chimneys, flues and vents for leakage and blockage by debris
  CSIA homeowner resources (NFPA 211, annual inspection): https://csia.org/homeowner-resources
  CSIA FAQ (annual inspections for all chimneys, gas included): https://csia.org/faq.html
  CPSC annual fuel-burning appliance inspection: https://www.cpsc.gov/content/cpsc-urges-annual-fuel-burning-appliance-inspection-to-prevent-deaths-fires
  NFPA heating safety tip sheet (Michigan copy): https://www.michigan.gov/documents/lara/NFPA._Heating_safety_tips._7._2019._MIPLogo._ECS._Final__663826_7.pdf
  Napoleon (fireplace maker) on annual gas fireplace inspection: https://www.napoleon.com/en/us/fireplaces/blog/why-gas-fireplaces-need-annual-inspections
Cut: burning pine causes creosote (extension sources disagree), damper/fireplace heats the house (done in c12), CO from gas logs (health angle).
