Facts (search excerpts; publisher pages not opened, wording kept soft):
1. Grease fire: Minnesota State Fire Chiefs Association, MSFCA Recipe Book for Kitchen Safety (https://www.lexingtonmn.gov/page/open/1209/0/MSFCA%20Recipe%20Book%20for%20Kitchen%20Safety.pdf): water explodes on contact with grease, put a lid on it, remove heat; metal lid not glass or plastic.
2. Tornado windows: NWS information via University of Illinois Extension (https://extension.illinois.edu/news-releases/know-whats-true-and-whats-not-about-tornadoes): "wastes valuable time and won't keep a tornado from ripping off a roof or throwing debris into a home".
3. Smoke alarm: NFPA guidance as reported by safetyandhealthmagazine.com and military safety releases: replace 10 years after manufacture date, test monthly.
4. CO alarm: CPSC (https://www.cpsc.gov/Safety-Education/Safety-Education-Centers/Carbon-Monoxide-Information-Center/CO-Alarms) every level and outside sleeping areas; City of Madison Fire (https://www.cityofmadison.com/fire/your-safety/carbon-monoxide-alarms/where-to-place-your-carbon-monoxide-alarms): CO weight close to air so mounting height does not matter.
5. Lightning: NWS https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-myths: Empire State Building about 23 hits a year.
