Facts (search excerpts, 7 Nov 2026; sherwin-williams.com / benjaminmoore.com / epa.gov page fetches blocked in the cloud network, wording kept soft):
1. Dry vs cure: latex touch-dry in about 1 hour (Benjamin Moore W625 data sheet "To Touch 1 Hour", Behr PR370 data sheet); Sherwin-Williams: acrylic and latex paints take two or three weeks to cure; Benjamin Moore FAQ "Full cure: 14 days" (https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/project-center/paint/how-to-make-paint-dry-faster, https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/interior-exterior-paints-stains/how-to-advice/painting-101/make-paint-dry-faster, https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/faq, https://media.benjaminmoore.com/WebServices/prod/assets/stage/datasheets/TDS_0625/20180807%20W625%20TDS%20US%20OKF.pdf).
2. Paint-and-primer still needs primer on some surfaces: Benjamin Moore "if you're working with a previously painted surface that is in good condition, you most likely do NOT need to prime", primer recommended where stains/rust present, drywall primer for new drywall; Sherwin-Williams: seal water/smoke/ink/grease stains with a primer-sealer, lightly sand glossy surfaces to a dull finish (https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/contractors/job-solutions/project-advice/choosing-best-paint-primer, https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/project-center/paint/how-to-paint-prep).
3. Flat is hardest to clean: Benjamin Moore "Stains are harder to remove from low-sheen paint, and constant rubbing or cleaning can result in burnishing" (https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/interior-exterior-paints-stains/how-to-advice/interiors/choosing-paint-finish, https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-us/interior-exterior-paints-stains/how-to-advice/painting-101/cleaning-painted-walls).
4. Paint down the drain: EPA recommends not pouring household hazardous waste down the drain, on the ground or into storm sewers; storm drains lead to local waters untreated (https://www.epa.gov/hw/household-hazardous-waste-hhw, https://cfpub.epa.gov/npstbx/files/sww_painting.pdf). Benjamin Moore: latex may be discarded in the trash once dried out (https://www.benjaminmoore.com/en-ca/interior-exterior-paints-stains/how-to-advice/painting-101/paint-disposal). California prohibits drying/trashing latex (https://calrecycle.ca.gov/paint/, https://www.paintcare.org/faqs/), hence "check local rules".
5. Cold weather: Sherwin-Williams exterior FAQ "Until a few years ago you needed to paint above 50° F. Sherwin-Williams now has products like Duration, Resilience, SuperPaint and A-100 Exterior that will allow you to paint down to 35° F" and warns against painting when nighttime lows drop below 35° F (https://www.sherwin-williams.com/painting-contractors/products/resources/faqs/exterior-product-application-faqs).
