c01-energy-myths: sources (checked 30 Sep 2026)

1. Thermostat: "Setting it higher won't make your home heat up any faster; it will only cause your system to overshoot the desired temperature." Trane, quoted in Popular Science, https://www.popsci.com/diy/home-energy-conservation-myths/
2. Fans cool people, not rooms (wind-chill effect; DOE says fans let you raise the thermostat about 4°F). Popular Science (same article), citing the U.S. Department of Energy.
3. Closing vents in unused rooms can increase energy use (duct pressure pushes air out through leaks). 2003 Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory study, via Popular Science (same article).
4. "The operating life of a LED is unaffected by turning it on and off." U.S. Department of Energy, quoted in Popular Science (same article).
5. "No, storage in a refrigerator or freezer is not required or recommended for batteries produced today." Recommended storage at room temperature, 68°F to 78°F. Energizer non-rechargeable battery FAQ, https://data.energizer.com/pdfs/non-rechargeable_faq.pdf
