Facts (search excerpts, 24 Oct 2026; publisher pages not opened, wording kept soft):
1. Mowing: one-third rule and 3 to 4 inches for tall fescue: University of Maryland Extension (https://extension.umd.edu/node/2405), University of Georgia Extension C 1031-2 (https://secure.caes.uga.edu/extension/publications/files/pdf/C 1031_2.PDF).
2. Clippings and thatch: extension grasscycling guides (e.g. https://www.lawrenceks.org/wp-content/uploads/modified-grasscyc17.pdf, K-State MF1155): grasscycling does not cause thatch; thatch can keep clippings from reaching the soil.
3. Watering: University of Wisconsin Extension (https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/lawn-maintenance/): about 1 inch a week, watering more often than once a week encourages shallow roots.
4. Leaves: University of Minnesota Extension (via https://www.audacy.com/kywnewsradio/news/national/u-of-mn-extension-says-mulching-leaves-is-best-route), Michigan State University Extension (https://www.canr.msu.edu/uploads/files/Mulch_fallen_leavesRS.pdf): wet, matted leaves smother grass; mulching with a mower is an option; MSU reports benefits from mulching leaves. The "up to six inches" figure was left out (secondary source).
5. Mulch volcanoes: UVM Extension (https://www.uvm.edu/extension/news/beware-mulch-volcano), University of Illinois Extension (https://extension.illinois.edu/blogs/merry-gardener/2018-04-09-volcano-mulch), University of Kentucky HO-106 (https://publications.ca.uky.edu/sites/publications.ca.uky.edu/files/ho106.pdf): 2 to 4 inches, not touching the trunk.
