Facts locked from search excerpts 19 Oct 2026 (publisher pages not opened):
- Hot food straight in the fridge, shallow containers 2 in, within 2 h, leftovers 3-4 days: CDC and USDA advice repeated by extension services (Cornell EFNEP newsletter, NC State Extension, Food Safety News).
- Freezing at 0F inactivates, not kills; USDA Campylobacter study (about 10% survived 1 week at -20C): K-State Extension "Freezing Your Food" citing USDA FSIS https://douglas.ksu.edu/docs/healthandnutrition/fuel_your_family/Freezing%20Your%20Food_Fuel%20Your%20Family.pdf
- Freezer burn is dryness, not a safety problem: same K-State handout.
- Smell/taste/look do not reveal harmful bacteria: FSIS archived page https://webharvest.gov/peth04/20041015025001/http://www.fsis.usda.gov/OA/pubs/focus_ref.htm ; CSPI https://www.cspinet.org/daily/food-safety/can-you-tell-if-a-food-is-safe-to-eat-by-smelling-it
- Fridge at 40F or below: Clemson HGIC citing USDA https://hgic.clemson.edu/?p=2655
