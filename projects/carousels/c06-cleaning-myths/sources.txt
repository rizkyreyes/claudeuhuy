c06-cleaning-myths sources (checked 12 Oct 2026 by web search excerpts; publisher pages not opened)
1. Flushable wipes: utilities say don't flush any wipes regardless of label; UF/IFAS lab comparison: toilet paper dissolved after 5 min soaking, wipes barely broke down after 24 h. https://nacwa.org/news-publications/news-detail/2020/02/13/toilets-are-not-a-trash-can-here-s-why-you-shouldn-t-flush-so-called-flushable-wipes ; https://blogs.ifas.ufl.edu/orangeco/2020/03/25/flushable-or-not/
2. Vinegar on natural stone: acid can etch marble/limestone, dull granite and degrade its sealer. https://www.hunker.com/1877925/countertops-avoid-use-vinegar-clean-granite-stone-marble ; https://www.realhomes.com/au/advice/what-not-to-clean-with-vinegar (commercial sources, secondary; kept to "can")
3. Mixing: bleach + ammonia gives chloramine, bleach + acid (incl. vinegar) gives chlorine gas; CDC advises not to mix cleaning products. https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/reduce-your-risk-toxic-gases-never-mix-bleach-other-cleaning-products (CDC page not opened)
4. Air fresheners: don't clean or freshen the air, they add chemicals; HEPA air cleaner for particles. https://www.umass.edu/ehs/air-fresheners
5. Disinfectant contact time: must stay visibly wet for the label contact time. https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/publications/p03748.pdf ; https://www.oregon.gov/oda/pesticides/pesticide-product-information/Documents/CandidaAurisDisinfectant.pdf (EPA label requirement for wipes only via trade article, so source line says "product labels")
