Facts (search excerpts, 3 Nov 2026; energy.gov not reachable from the cloud network, wording kept soft, no extra numbers):
1. Thermostat setback: DOE Energy Saver thermostats page (https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/programmable-thermostats, excerpt via https://www.energy.gov/es/energysaver/thermostats): "as much as 10% a year on heating and cooling by simply turning your thermostat back 7-10°F for 8 hours a day"; DOE calls the "works harder to reheat" belief a misconception (quoted by https://clark.com/personal-finance-credit/thermostat-heat-temperature-setting-best-winter/, https://www.thisoldhouse.com/heating-cooling/how-thermostat-setbacks-save-money). c01 busted "crank it up to heat faster", this is a different myth.
2. Space heater in a wall outlet, not a power strip / extension cord: CPSC and American Red Cross via KHOU Verify (https://www.khou.com/article/news/verify/weather-verify/do-not-plug-space-heaters-into-extension-cords-power-strips-fire-safety/536-71d41380-6c62-438c-9dfd-ca4b087a4bde), PIRG (https://pirg.org/edfund/resources/space-heaters-lead-to-1700-fires-and-80-deaths-a-year-here-are-tips-to-stay-safe/), NC State Extension (https://camden.ces.ncsu.edu/2026/01/space-heater-safety).
3. Off when leaving the room or sleeping; 3 feet from anything that can burn: NFPA and Red Cross via the same KHOU Verify piece; PIRG.
4. Open fireplace: EPA via https://www.bobvila.com/interior/fireplace-efficiency.md (draft pulls warm air up the chimney), DOE Energy Saver fireplace article (https://www.Energy.gov/energysaver/articles/brrr-5-ways-maximize-your-fireplace-and-chimney-efficiency), damper closed between fires (https://fireplaceuniverse.com/do-fireplaces-lose-heat/). The "90 %" figure was left out (secondary quote).
5. Cold-climate heat pumps: ENERGY STAR cold-climate certification, NYS Clean Heat (https://cleanheat.ny.gov/heat-pumps-cold-climates-do-they-work), Massachusetts fact sheet (https://www.swampscottma.gov/sites/g/files/vyhlif1296/f/uploads/ma_cold_climate_heat-pumps_factsheet.pdf), DOE challenge fact sheet (https://www.energy.gov/eere/buildings/articles/residential-cold-climate-heat-pump-technology-challenge-fact-sheet). Vendor low-temperature numbers left out.
No myth overlaps an upcoming 3D video (water heater, oil rigs, P-trap).
