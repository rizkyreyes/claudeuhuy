Facts (search excerpts, 11 Nov 2026; source pages could not be fetched from the cloud network, so wording is kept soft; no health claims):
1. AWD doesn't help you stop: Consumer Reports tire expert: stopping and cornering are where all-wheel drive offers nothing; winter tires give the best grip for going, stopping and cornering; winter tires matter more than AWD in many situations (https://www.consumerreports.org/cro/magazine/2015/09/do-you-really-need-awd-in-the-snow/index.htm). Bridgestone "debunks four winter driving myths": 4WD helps a vehicle get moving but doesn't improve stopping or cornering (https://www.equipmentworld.com/trucks/pickups/article/15043502/bridgestone-debunks-four-winter-driving-myths; Bridgestone 2020 survey listing "four-wheel drive makes stopping in snow more effective" as a common myth: https://bridgestoneamericas.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2020/bridgestone-survey-winter-driving).
2. Underinflating for snow traction: Bridgestone: "Never reduce tire pressures in an attempt to increase traction on snow or ice", deflating "simply doesn't work", and driving under-inflated can damage tires (same Bridgestone release above; https://www2020.theautochannel.com/news/2013/11/27/097293-bridgestone-debunks-four-winter-driving-myths.html). Michelin's technical communications director called it "a terrible myth" (https://www2020.theautochannel.com/news/2022/10/20/1207353-5-myths-about-winter-tires.html).
3. Hot water on a windshield: AAA Northeast: throwing hot water on a cold windshield makes the glass expand very quickly, which can crack it; use the defroster and let the glass warm up gradually (https://magazine.northeast.aaa.com/daily/life/cars-trucks/how-to-defrost-car-windows/; https://news.yahoo.com/fact-check-pouring-hot-water-222425664.html).
4. Pumping ABS brakes: NHTSA winter driving tips: "If you have antilock brakes, apply firm, continuous pressure to the brake pedal" (pumping is only for non-ABS cars) (https://www.nhtsa.gov/winter-driving-tips; https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.gov/files/documents/winter-driving-tips.pdf). NHTSA-hosted report: the ABS does its own "pumping"; pumping the pedal can defeat it (https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/13731/dot_13731_DS1.pdf).
5. Cold and tire pressure: AAA: "Tire pressure decreases about 1 PSI for every 10-degree Fahrenheit drop in temperature" (https://cluballiance.aaa.com/the-extra-mile/advice/car/how-temperature-changes-affect-your-tire-pressure; CBS citing AAA: https://cbsnews.com/texas/news/weather-101-cold-air-tire-pressure). Recommended pressure is on the driver's door jamb placard (FMVSS 110, see c04).
Skipped on purpose: engine warm-up (c04), idling, premium gas, oil change. No myth overlaps an upcoming 3D video (pipes under rivers, lightning rods, pile drivers, sewers, runways). "Bridges freeze first" not used (it's true, not a myth).
