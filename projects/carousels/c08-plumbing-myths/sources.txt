c08-plumbing-myths sources (checked 17 Oct 2026 by web search excerpts; publisher pages not opened)
1. Ice and disposals: disposals use lugs/impellers on a spinning plate, not sharp blades; ice may dislodge debris but doesn't sharpen. https://www.moen.com/press-room/articles/2016aug_disposalmyths (Moen press material; secondary syndicated copies agree)
2. Cold water with the disposal: keeps fats solid; hot water can let grease slip further down and clog downstream. Same Moen page.
3. Water heater maintenance: sediment buildup reduces efficiency and shortens life; flush about once a year. https://www.serviceexperts.com/blog/plumbing-myths-debunking-10-common-misconceptions-and-mistakes ; https://www.rotorooter.com/blog/3-plumbing-myths/ (trade sources, secondary; source line says "plumbing industry guidance")
4. Tank tablets: plumbers say chlorine tablets can wear rubber flapper/seals and a shrinking tablet can lodge in the flush valve. https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/diy-plumbing-solutions-to-avoid-36998249 ; https://www.aol.com/why-plumbers-warn-against-using-170000949.html (anecdotal/trade; wording kept to "can")
5. Pipes in unheated areas can freeze even in a warm house; open sink cabinets and drip a faucet in a hard freeze: AP-based story on local news https://whyy.org/articles/frozen-pipes-prevention-cold-weather/ , Angi https://www.angi.com/articles/how-to-drip-faucets.htm (Ready.gov / NWS pages not opened)
