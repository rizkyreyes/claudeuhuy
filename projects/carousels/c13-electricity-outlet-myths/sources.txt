Facts (search excerpts, 5 Nov 2026; energy.gov / esfi.org / cpsc.gov page fetches blocked in the cloud network, wording kept soft):
1. Standby power: chargers and other plugged-in products keep drawing power; estimated 5-10% of residential electricity (DOE / Berkeley Lab figures quoted by Scientific American https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/plugged-in/vampires-in-your-house, Carolina Country co-op magazine https://www.carolinacountry.com/your-energy/the-price-of-standby-mode, LBNL worldwide review 3-12 % https://www.osti.gov/biblio/795343). Dollar figures left out (sources differ).
2. Power strip vs surge protector: basic strips only add outlets; surge protectors carry a joule rating and a UL 1449 listing (https://www.bobvila.com/articles/surge-protector-vs-power-strip.md, https://www.lowes.com/n/buying-guide/surge-protector-buying-guide, https://www.slashgear.com/1831280/power-strip-vs-surge-protector-identify).
3. Surge protectors wear out: protective parts degrade with surges, some keep passing power after protection is gone, replace when the "protected" light is out (https://www.pemco.com/blog/replace-surge-protectors-every-two-years, https://www.jaspector.com/wiki/surge-protector/, https://www.lowes.com/n/buying-guide/surge-protector-buying-guide). Lifespan years left out (sources differ).
4. Cords under rugs: CPSC "Do not run extension cords under carpets, through doorways or under furniture" / "If the cord is covered, heat cannot escape, which can result in fire" (https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/5032.pdf, https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20121002041218/http://www.cpsc.gov/cpscpub/pubs/513.pdf).
5. Don't oversize breakers/fuses: the device is sized to the wire; never replace with a higher rating (CPSC + NESF checklist https://corpora.tika.apache.org/base/docs/govdocs1/191/191928.html, https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2000/CPSC-and-NESF-Urge-Consumers-to-Plug-Into-Electrical-Safety, Indiana Electric Cooperatives https://www.indianaconnection.org/breakers-fuses-101/).
No myth overlaps an upcoming 3D video (canal locks, ships, suspension bridge, water tower, nuclear plant, sewers, runways). c12 already did "space heater in a power strip"; not repeated.
