## YouTube
Title: Why Does Your Running Shoe Have an Extra Hole at the Top? #shorts

Description:
That extra eyelet is for a heel lock, also called a runner's loop. Lace up to the second to last hole, run each lace up through the last hole on its side to make a small loop, then cross the laces and thread each end through the opposite loop before you tie. It cinches the top of the shoe so your heel stays put, which means less rubbing and fewer blisters. If it still slips, the shoe may just not fit.
