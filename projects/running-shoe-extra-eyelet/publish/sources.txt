- Extra top eyelet is used for the heel lock / runner's loop (lace lock) to limit heel slippage; method: lace normally to the second to last eyelet, thread each lace up through the last eyelet on the same side to form a small loop, cross the laces and thread each through the opposite loop, pull and tie: Road Runner Sports https://www.roadrunnersports.com/blog/how-to-prevent-heel-slippage-in-running-shoes.md , Salomon https://www.salomon.com/en-us/sg/a/how-to-tie-trail-running-shoes-best-lacing-techniques , Coach https://www.coachweb.com/gear/running-gear/heel-lock-lacing-how-to-tie-a-runners-knot-to-stop-your-heel-from-slipping , Nike https://www.nike.com/a/running-shoe-lacing-techniques
