The designer of the original PlayStation controller, Teiyu Goto, picked the shapes for meaning. Triangle is a point of view, square is a sheet of paper for menus and documents, and circle and X are yes and no. In Japan a circle means correct and an X means wrong, so games there confirmed with circle. Western versions flipped it, so you press X to say yes.
