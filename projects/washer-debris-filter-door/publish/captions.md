YouTube description:
Behind that little door is the washer's debris filter. It catches coins, hairclips, lint and hair before they reach the drain pump. When it clogs, the washer can't drain properly. Unplug the machine, put down a towel and a tub, drain the leftover water, then turn the filter counterclockwise to pull it out and rinse it.
